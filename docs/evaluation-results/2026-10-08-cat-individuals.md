## Threats to validity

1. Photos came from one Kaggle benchmark, not verified independent outdoor encounter days or Android devices. File assignment to reference/development/holdout was synthetic rather than chronological.
2. Multi-cat photos were excluded rather than passed through manual crop choice. This overstates coverage of the real user workflow and is already visible in the 11 failure count.
3. The false-positive estimate comes from just 16 cat-absent scenarios, which are derived from the same holdout photos and not independent wild unknown cats. Observing zero errors in the first-only variant does **not** establish a negligible real-world error rate.
4. This experiment must not be retuned on Q003. For future progress, collect or reserve genuinely new cat identities/images, prespecify strategy/threshold, repeat the cat-absent tests, and add outdoor Android validation.
